FONTLOG for Eugene music font

Eugene is a SMuFL-compliant OpenType music font inspired by the engraving style of E. Fromont
publications.
The font is licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1.

https://github.com/mikkopatama/eugenefont
