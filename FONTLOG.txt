FONTLOG for Eugene music font

Eugene is a SMuFL-compliant OpenType music font inspired by the engraving style of E. Fromont
publications.
The font is licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1.

https://github.com/mikkopatama/eugenefont


Change log
----------

9 May 2026 (v. 0.505)

- Modified:
	- Ligatures, so that Musescore 4.6 will now also load the compound dynamic symbols like ff and pp correctly.
	- Quarter and half noteheads: the shapes are now closer to Fromont references and the notehead anchors are set correctly in the metadata


Added:
	Added EugeneText.otf, mostly BravuraText.otf glyphs for now but will update later with wider coverage.

7 May 2026 (v. 0.504)

- Created a pdf list of all currently available glyphs, replacing "Glyph list.txt"

- Added
	U+E007–E009  System dividers
	U+E263	Double sharp
	U+E265	Triple sharp
	U+E266	Triple flat
	U+E267	Natural flat
	U+E268	Natural sharp
	U+E269	Sharp sharp
	
	U+F408  French style C clef, as found on Prélude à "l'Après-midi d'un faune" from 1895
	U+E4CC-E4CD  Short fermata above and below

- Modified:
	- Several time signature numbers are now much more balanced and consistent in style
	- Ottava number 8 was redrawn from a better reference, updated all glyphs that include 8, including clefs and ottava signs
	- Ottava alta and ottava bassa got a new letter "a", similar to E. Fromont editions which either use just "8" or "8a" for ottava signs
	- All p and f signs got a new look
	- mezzo m is not as weird anymore
